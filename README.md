## The entry

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network, deterministic) writes one
original six-word story to `/output/entry.txt`:

> **He finally forgave himself. Too late.**

A man spends his life carrying a guilt he cannot set down. At the very
end he finally forgives himself — and the last two words turn the whole
thing: forgiveness arrives, but too late for whoever he wronged, or for the
life he could have lived. A whole life is implied in six words, and what he
did — and why it is too late — is left for the reader to fill in.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
